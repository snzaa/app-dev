	** ** # One Piece
1. One Piece is a Japanese manga series created by Eiichiro Oda, first serialized in 1997.
2. The story follows a main character named Monkey D. Luffy who has the ambition to become the King of the Pirates
3. Its power system consists of devil fruits which can give them powers based on the properties/definition of the fruit and Haki (3 Kinds) that defies the devil fruits.
4. He forms a crew called the Straw Hat Pirates, each member with unique abilities, dreams, and backstories.
5. The crew travels across dangerous seas, facing rival pirates, powerful marines, and mysterious creatures.
